Geometry

How the foil was skipped

  • The GEMs and trigger were daughters of a 50 × 30 × 20 cm vacuum box centred on the top GEM.
  • The exit chamber, foil included, is placed in the World. The foil is 49.7 mm below the top GEM: inside the box.
  • Geant4 has no overlapping siblings: a particle from the dipole crosses the box surface first, and inside the box only the box's daughters exist.
  • The box was placed without an overlap check, and g4DL silences overlap warnings (g4DL.cc).

Evidence

Why change the material instead of removing the window

  • Placing a CAD part runs an overlap check that draws from the main random engine: removing any part shifts every event's random sequence. Reseeding after /run/initialize removes that.
  • Removing the window (reseeded): left arm identical, 3,705 of 3,705 (left).
  • Right arm: identical until event 8,619. A 0.13 MeV photon at (−167, −51, 520) mm, inside the 6.4 mm overlap of the PEEK and steel chamber meshes, is put in the PEEK with the window and in the steel without it: the World's navigation voxels change. Nothing there touches the foil.
  • Changing only the material keeps the geometry: identical on both arms.

New geometry: the foil now acts

With Al, 21 % fewer electrons reach the left bottom GEM (2,892 vs 3,677) and 7 % fewer on the right (7,221 vs 7,806). Both bands broaden; the left band's peak also moves, from about −30 to −40 mm. The per-event panels are not a per-event foil effect: once the foil scatters a particle the two random sequences part.
